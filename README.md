### Pollux Gronier

Student in the ESSEC & CentraleSupélec BSc in Artificial Intelligence, Data and Management Sciences (AIDAMS). I like building complete data systems, from scraping and storage to models and a usable interface, mostly around finance and energy.

**Personal projects**

- [Peatrading](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/Peatrading): daily stock screening and risk engine for a French PEA account. Euronext data, signals (mean reversion, pairs, HMM regimes, XGBoost), a chain of risk checks, Streamlit dashboard, FastAPI and Discord alerts. About 140 tests, CI.
- [Cdm-analysis](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/Cdm-analysis): 2026 World Cup predictions. Elo, Poisson and gradient boosting blended with bookmaker odds, Monte Carlo tournament simulation, DuckDB, Streamlit.
- [vintedbot](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/vintedbot): finds second-hand deals across six marketplaces, with anti-bot handling, rule-based filtering, spec extraction and scoring. 138 tests.
- [application-assistant](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/application-assistant): n8n Discord bot that tailors my CV and cover letter to a job offer, with Postgres and Google Docs.
- [Trading_bot](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/Trading_bot): weekly ETF rotation strategy on an Alpaca paper account, Dockerized with a dashboard.

**Course projects**

- [nuclear-capital-risk](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/nuclear-capital-risk): extending old nuclear reactors vs building new ones, cost and CO₂ model.
- [Environemental-economics-](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/Environemental-economics-): 64 years of climate data for 12 countries and panel regressions on growth.
- [Monopoly](https://github.com/Polluxgnr/Monopoly): Monte Carlo and Markov chain to find the most profitable properties.

**Tools:** Python (pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, statsmodels), SQL (DuckDB, SQLite, Postgres), Streamlit, FastAPI, Docker, Git, n8n.
